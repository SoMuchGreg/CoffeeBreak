With Phase 2 on the horizon, we wanted to share how we go about forming rosters and managing the bench, so you understand what our thought process is and why we're making the decisions we make.

## Ranks
You may have noticed we have different guild ranks. With Phase 2 here, we'd like to start putting real meaning behind these. Many of you have been with us a long time and show up to raids week after week. That kind of dedication doesn't go unnoticed, and we want to recognise it properly - without you, we wouldn't be able to form raids week in, week out. Starting with Phase 2, when there are more signups than raid spots, **Raiders will be given preference**. Members are absolutely still welcome - we love having you. It's just that when we have to choose, we wouldn't feel right turning away someone who's put in so much effort over time. And to make sure Members are always part of the raids too, we've built in a small reservation that keeps a couple of spots open for them at every raid - more on this below.

Ranks aren't set in stone - they can move both ways. They're based on your **signup rate**: how often you as an individual have signed up since you joined us. The window starts at *your* first signup, so newer members aren't measured against anyone else's longer history. Sign up consistently and you'll move up over time; if you go quiet for a long stretch, the rate will reflect that. One thing worth knowing: it's *signups* we count - if you get benched by us (no self-benching - just go *absent* if you can't make it), we still count that as a signup.

### How we decided the current ranks
For the current assignments, here's what we looked at:
- **Signup rate over time** - roughly half of raids signed up for is enough. You don't need to have made every one.
- **Long unannounced absences** - stretches where someone went quiet without telling us makes planning so much harder.
- **Three or more no-shows** - signing up and then not showing up at all without letting us know.

None of these were hard cutoffs - they're the starting points we used to shape things.

## Keeping us posted
Managing a guild isn't the easiest thing in the world, so we'd ask that you do just a couple of things to help us out. The first is timely signups: we start composing the roster roughly 48 hours before the raid, and signups that come in later than that might not make it into this one - late additions can be tricky to fit in. The earlier you sign up, the easier it is to make a spot for you.

The second is letting us know about longer breaks. If you'll be away from the game for a while, please give us a heads-up. Without one, we're left guessing whether you'll be back - and that makes longer-term roster planning much harder.

## What a raid is shaped like
Our 25-man raids (Gruul+Mag, SSC, Tempest Keep) mostly aim for **3 Tanks, 6 Healers, and 16 DPS**. Tank slots go to our designated **Core tanks**; healer and DPS slots fill from the signup pool.

## Three core principles
Everything we do when forming a roster follows from three rules:
1. **Fair rotation.** When more people sign up than there are spots, the bench is shared so that everyone takes their turn on the bench., to ensure that no-one is benched multiple raids in a row.
2. **Mainspec over offspec.** When the raid is short a role, we bring in someone whose main already covers it rather than ask another player to switch off their main.
3. **Raid spot priority.** Officers and Core tanks have first call on spots, then Raiders, then Members - and a small carve-out makes sure Members always get a chance to play.

The rest of this document expands each of these in turn.

## Fair rotation
We know sitting out a raid isn't fun. What you might not see is how much work goes on behind the scenes to keep things fair and to minimise how often each of you ends up on the bench. We're a casual guild - we don't require any attendance percentage, and we'd rather you put life first than feel forced to show up. The flip side is that we like having a few more signups than raid spots, because that gives us some contingency when unexpected things happen. The principle behind that rotation is simple: everyone shares the bench in roughly equal measure. We track it carefully and distribute the weight as evenly as we possibly can. If you sit out tonight, it isn't bad luck - it just means it's your turn, and it will be someone else's next time. Some amount of benching is just a normal part of raiding with us in Coffee Break - what we promise as officers is that we'll always do our absolute best to keep it fair.
There are a few points to note about this:
- **Each raid location keeps its own bench count.** Karazhan, Gruul+Mag, SSC, and Tempest Keep are tracked separately - sitting at one location doesn't shield you from sitting at another.
- **Healers rotate among healers; tanks and DPS rotate among themselves.** A healer's count isn't compared to a DPS - they're not competing for the same spots.
- **Every bench counts equally.** Every time anyone sits out, it adds to their bench count the same way as any other bench. This is what makes the rotation real: without it, the same player could be picked to sit again and again with no consequence.

## Mainspec over offspec
Every player has a **mainspec** - the role you've played the most with us, the one you've indicated you want to play, and the one we're counting on you for.
The principle: when the raid is short a role, we'd rather bring in someone whose mainspec already covers it than ask another player to switch off their main.
We apply this rule as follows: a Member healer-main can take a healer slot ahead of a Raider DPS-main when the raid is short a healer. The Member is filling the slot as their mainspec; the Raider would have been playing offspec.

### Composition spec flex - when we ask you to play your offspec
When a role is short and no mainspec player covers it, we ask **dual-spec players** to switch to their offspec - a **composition spec flex** - starting with **priority offspec** (those that we've highlighted in a previous announcement) players.

## Raid spot priority
Every player has a rank:
- **Officers and Core tanks** - always in the raid if they signed up. They are never benched by any of the rules, but officers will voluntarily bench where appropriate.
- **Raiders** - fill the spots that remain after Officers and Core tanks. If too many Raiders sign up, fair rotation decides who sits.
- **Members** - invited after the others, with a couple of spots always set aside for them (next section).

This order generally holds, with two exceptions: the Mainspec over offspec rule (above) and the Member Reservation rule (below).

### Two spots per raid reserved for Members
We've set aside 2 spots for Members in every raid - if no Members sign, this will be filled with raiders; otherwise we'll take 23 Officers/Raiders and 2 Members. The idea behind this is we want to encourage newer or casual players to continue to sign with us, without being discouraged by us having a "raider core", while still making sure we reserve places for those who are filling our raids week in, week out.
